# Pricing

Pricing model and packaging for the Operational Efficiency Dashboard.

---

## Pricing Model

**Type:** Standalone monthly subscription, sold separately from core AP automation

### Pricing Structure

| Component | Calculation | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Base | 10% of customer's core Ottimate MRR | Scales with customer size |
| Floor | $200/month minimum | Ensures viability for smaller accounts |
| Cap | $500-750/month maximum | Makes product accessible vs. enterprise competitors |

### Examples

| Customer Ottimate MRR | Dashboard Price | Notes |
|----------------------|-----------------|-------|
| $1,500/month | $200/month | Floor applies |
| $2,500/month | $250/month | 10% calculation |
| $4,000/month | $400/month | 10% calculation |
| $6,000/month | $600/month | 10% calculation |
| $10,000/month | $750/month | Cap applies |

### Rationale

- **10% of MRR:** Value scales with customer size and usage
- **$200 floor:** Below this, economics don't work; filters for serious customers
- **$500-750 cap:** Makes enterprise deals attractive; competitors charge $100K+/year

## Design Partner Pricing

**Purpose:** Incentivize early adoption, feedback, and case study participation

| Option | Price | Requirements |
|--------|-------|--------------|
| Free (6 months) | $0 | Weekly feedback calls, case study, 3 reference calls |
| 50% discount (6 months) | 50% of calculated price | Monthly feedback, testimonial quote, 1 reference call |

### Design Partner Commitment

Partners must agree to:
1. Dedicated contact for feedback sessions
2. Access to metrics for case study development
3. Testimonial quote approval
4. Reference call availability
5. Product feedback within 48 hours of requests

### Transition to Paid

After 6-month design partner period:
- Partners convert to full pricing
- Loyalty discount of 10% for first year (optional)
- No contract commitment required

- My Tasks panel

**Insight Engine:**
- AI-powered insight generation

- Mentor assignment with email sending

- Vendor improvement workflows

- Task management

**Reporting:**
- All report templates (Executive Summary, QBR, Location, Vendor, Team)

- Scheduled email reports

- Activity log access
- Historical data access
- Inferred impact tracking

## Sales Guidance

### Qualification Questions

1. Do you have multiple locations? (Multi-location = higher value)
2. How do you currently track AP team performance? (Manual = pain point)
3. What visibility do you have into location differences? (None = opportunity)
4. How do you identify training needs? (Ad hoc = opportunity)
5. How long does it take to compile AP reports? (Hours = pain point)

### Objection Handling

**"We already have reports"**
> "Do your current reports tell you what to DO about the issues they show? Our dashboard doesn't just visualize data—it recommends specific actions and tracks outcomes."

**"This seems expensive for a dashboard"**
> "Let's look at the math. If we identify one underperformer costing $2,000/month in productivity loss, and help you address it, the dashboard pays for itself in the first month."

**"We can build this ourselves"**
> "You could, but consider: we've built pre-configured insight rules, action workflows, and report templates specifically for AP. Building this internally typically takes 6-12 months and requires ongoing maintenance."

**"We need to see ROI first"**
> "That's exactly why we have a design partner program. Join us for 6 months, get full access, and you'll have concrete ROI data before committing to paid."

### Value Quantification

Help prospects calculate potential value:

1. **Staff productivity gap:** 
   - Identify 1 underperformer 20% below average
   - 20% gap × 8 hours/day × $25/hour × 22 days = $880/month
   
2. **Exception reduction:**
   - Reduce exceptions by 25% through training
   - 100 exceptions/month × 25% × 15 min × $25/hour = $156/month

3. **Report time savings:**
   - Director spends 4 hours/month on manual reports
   - 4 hours × $75/hour = $300/month

**Total example value:** $1,336/month vs. $400 dashboard cost = 3.3x ROI

## Billing & Contracts

### Billing

- Monthly billing, no annual commitment required
- Invoice on 1st of month for upcoming month
- Net 30 payment terms
- Credit card or ACH accepted

### Contract Terms

- Month-to-month by default
- Annual prepay: 10% discount
- Cancellation: 30 days notice
- Data retention: 90 days after cancellation

### Upsell Path

Future premium features (not in V1):
- Custom report builder
- External integrations
- Advanced analytics / predictive insights
- Multi-company rollup (for holding companies)
