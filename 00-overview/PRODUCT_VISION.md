# Product Vision

## One-Liner

AI-powered operational intelligence that tells AP teams exactly where to focus and what to do about it.

## The Problem

AP teams have data but not direction. Reports show what happened, not what to do. Managers spend hours in spreadsheets trying to answer basic questions:

- Who on my team needs help?
- Which location is falling behind?
- Which vendors are causing problems?
- What's all this costing us?

Current state:
- Data exists in Ottimate but requires manual analysis
- No proactive identification of issues
- Best practices stuck in silos (Austin does great, LA struggles, nobody knows why)
- Executives get inconsistent, manually-compiled reports
- No connection between "we identified a problem" and "here's what we did about it"

## The Solution

A dashboard that delivers the **Insights → Recommendations → Actions** flow:

### 1. Surfaces Insights
Not just metrics, but prioritized recommendations with cost impact. "John Miller is processing 20% below team average, costing you $840/month in lost productivity."

### 2. Enables Action
Pre-built workflows for common interventions:
- Assign a mentor to struggling staff
- Generate training recommendations with supporting data
- Start vendor improvement workflows with email templates
- Set benchmark targets based on top performers

### 3. Tracks Outcomes
Connects actions to results over time:
- "Since GL training initiated on March 15, LA's GL exceptions decreased 23%"
- Historical record of every action taken
- Inferred impact correlation (not causation, but valuable signal)

### 4. Reports Upward
Executive-ready summaries that translate operations into dollars:
- One-page monthly summary
- Quarterly business reviews with ROI waterfalls
- Scheduled email digests
- Always answer: "Are we getting better or worse? Where's the money going?"

## Core Principles

### 1. Every Number Links to Source
Click any metric → see the invoices behind it. No more "the data says X" without proof. Two clicks from KPI to individual invoice.

### 2. Costs Over Percentages
Translate everything to dollars using $25/hour AP cost. Not "12.4% exception rate" but "12.4% exception rate = $3,200/month in rework."

### 3. Actions, Not Just Alerts
Every insight has a recommended next step. Not "John is struggling" but "John is struggling → here's a performance review template → here are mentor options → track the outcome."

### 4. Internal Benchmarking
Compare against your own best performers. "Austin does it this way, so can LA." No debates about external benchmark validity.

### 5. Historical Accountability
Track what was done and what resulted. Build institutional memory. Enable "what did we try last time?" questions.

## What This Is Not

| This Product | Not This |
|--------------|----------|
| Operational intelligence layer | Replacement for core AP automation |
| Recommends training needs | Learning management system |
| Creates tasks with context | Project management tool |
| Tracks vendor outreach | Vendor portal for invoice submission |
| Generates email templates | Email marketing platform |
| Identifies calendar-worthy events | Calendar application |

We stay in our lane: **insight + recommendation + template + tracking**. Users execute in their existing tools.

## Competitive Positioning

We're not competing with:
- Generic BI tools (Tableau, Power BI) — we're purpose-built for AP
- AP automation vendors' basic reporting — we're operational intelligence, not just charts

We win because:
- AI-powered recommendations, not just visualizations
- Pre-built action workflows specific to AP operations
- Cost translation built in ($25/hr assumption)
- Drill-down to invoice level
- Action → outcome tracking
