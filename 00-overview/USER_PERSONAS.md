# User Personas

## Overview

Four distinct user types with different needs, workflows, and success criteria. The dashboard must serve all of them, but priorities differ by persona.

| Persona | Frequency | Primary Tab | Key Action |
|---------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| AP Manager | Daily | Staff Performance | Assign mentor, prep reviews |
| Regional Manager | Weekly | Locations | Compare locations, set targets |
| Director of Finance | Weekly/Monthly | Overview + Locations | Review insights, export reports |
| Executive (CFO/VP) | Monthly | Reports | Generate executive summary |

---

## Primary Persona: AP Manager

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Titles** | AP Manager, AP Supervisor, AP Team Lead |
| **Reports to** | Controller, Director of Finance |
| **Team size** | 3-15 processors |
| **Experience** | 5-15 years in AP, promoted from processor |
| **Tech comfort** | Moderate; uses Ottimate daily, Excel weekly |

### Goals

1. **Performance Management:** Ensure team meets processing targets
2. **Early Intervention:** Identify and coach underperformers before problems escalate
3. **Exception Reduction:** Lower exception rates through training and process improvement
4. **Prove Value:** Demonstrate team's value to leadership with data

### Daily Workflow

| Time | Activity | Current Pain |
|------|----------|--------------|
| 8:00 AM | Check exception queue | No visibility into who's causing most exceptions |
| 9:00 AM | Assign work, answer questions | Don't know who's overloaded vs. underperforming |
| Ongoing | Handle escalations | Reactive, not proactive |
| 4:00 PM | Review day's progress | Manual tracking in spreadsheets |
| Weekly | 1:1s with team | No objective data for conversations |
| Monthly | Report to leadership | Hours compiling data manually |

### Pain Points

1. **No Individual Visibility:** Team averages hide individual performance. Can't tell if someone is struggling until it's a crisis.
2. **Reactive Management:** Discovers problems after they've become complaints or audit findings.
3. **Manual Reporting:** Spends 4-6 hours monthly compiling performance data for leadership.
4. **Subjective Coaching:** Performance conversations lack objective data; feels unfair to team.
5. **Training Justification:** Can't objectively prove training needs to get budget approval.

### Success Statement

> "I can see exactly who needs help and have the data to back up my coaching conversations."

### Key Features for This Persona

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Staff Performance tab | See individual metrics at a glance |
| Performance categories | Quickly identify who needs attention |
| Drill-down to invoices | Investigate specific issues |
| Training recommendations | Data-backed training requests |
| Performance review prep | Save hours on 1:1 preparation |
| Assign mentor | Easy intervention for struggling staff |

### Objections to Address

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already know who's struggling" | But do you have the data to prove it? And do you know the root cause? |
| "My team will feel watched" | Data enables fair treatment—no more subjective assessments |
| "I don't have time for another tool" | This saves 4-6 hours monthly on reporting alone |

## Secondary Persona: Director of Finance

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Titles** | Director of Finance, Controller, VP Finance |
| **Reports to** | CFO |
| **Scope** | Multiple locations, 20-100+ AP staff |
| **Experience** | 15+ years in finance, strategic focus |
| **Tech comfort** | High; evaluates and approves tools |

### Goals

1. **Cost Reduction:** Reduce AP operational costs measurably
2. **Consistency:** Ensure all locations meet standards
3. **ROI Justification:** Prove AP investment value to CFO/board
4. **Best Practice Replication:** Spread what works across organization

### Workflow

| Frequency | Activity | Current Pain |
|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Weekly | Glance at high-level metrics | No single view across locations |
| Monthly | Review location performance | Data comes from different sources, inconsistent |
| Monthly | Approve initiatives | Hard to prioritize without cost impact data |
| Quarterly | Present to leadership | Manual report compilation |

### Pain Points

1. **No Location Comparison:** Can't easily compare Austin vs. LA vs. Chicago. Each reports differently.
2. **Reliance on Self-Reporting:** Managers report what they want to report. No objective view.
3. **Can't Quantify Costs:** Knows there are inefficiencies but can't put a dollar figure on them.
4. **Siloed Best Practices:** Austin is great, but nobody knows why or how to replicate it.
5. **Reactive Strategy:** Only learns about problems when they become crises.

### Success Statement

> "I can show the CFO exactly how much we're saving and where the opportunities are."

### Key Features for This Persona

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Overview with ROI summary | Immediate answer to "how are we doing?" |
| Location comparison | Side-by-side performance analysis |
| Cost impact on insights | Everything translated to dollars |
| Benchmark targets | Set goals based on best performers |
| Location comparison reports | Shareable analysis |
| Executive summary export | Board-ready in one click |

### Objections to Address

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have reports" | Do they show you where to focus and what it's costing you? |
| "My managers should handle this" | This gives you visibility without micromanaging |
| "How do I know the data is accurate?" | It's the same data from Ottimate—just analyzed |

## Tertiary Persona: Regional Manager

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Titles** | Regional AP Manager, Area Controller |
| **Reports to** | Director of Finance |
| **Scope** | 3-10 locations in a geographic region |
| **Experience** | 8-12 years, promoted from AP Manager |
| **Tech comfort** | Moderate to high |

### Goals

1. **Hit Regional Targets:** Meet efficiency goals for the region
2. **Support Location Managers:** Help struggling locations improve
3. **Escalate Appropriately:** Know when to involve leadership
4. **Spread Success:** Replicate wins across the region

### Workflow

| Frequency | Activity | Current Pain |
|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Daily | Monitor regional exceptions | Checking multiple reports/dashboards |
| Weekly | Check location standings | Manual comparison |
| Weekly | Support struggling locations | Don't know which to prioritize |
| Monthly | Regional performance review | Compile data from multiple sources |

### Pain Points

1. **Data Scattered:** Each location has its own reports; no unified view.
2. **Pattern Blindness:** Hard to see trends across locations.
3. **No Playbook:** When a location struggles, no standard intervention approach.
4. **Time Wasted:** Spends hours each week just getting visibility.

### Success Statement

> "I can focus on my region and know exactly which location needs my attention this week."

### Key Features for This Persona

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Location filter | See only my region |
| Date range filter | Focus on relevant period |
| Location scorecard | Quick health check per location |
| Cross-location insights | "Pattern detected across Denver and Seattle" |
| Benchmark targets | Set goals relative to best regional performer |
| Location comparison report | Share with location managers |

## Executive Consumer: CFO/VP Finance

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Titles** | CFO, VP Finance, SVP Operations |
| **Scope** | Entire organization |
| **Time available** | 5-10 minutes for AP topics |
| **Need** | Answers, not data |

### Goals

1. **Trend Understanding:** Are we getting better or worse?
2. **Cost Visibility:** What is AP costing us?
3. **ROI Validation:** Is our AP investment paying off?
4. **Board Readiness:** Have answers when asked

### Workflow

| Frequency | Activity | Current Pain |
|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Monthly | 5-minute summary review | Gets inconsistent reports |
| Quarterly | Board prep | Can't trace claims to data |
| Ad-hoc | Answer leadership questions | Has to ask Director to compile data |

### Pain Points

1. **Inconsistent Reports:** Different format every month, hard to track trends.
2. **Can't Verify Claims:** Told "we're improving" but no proof.
3. **No Forward View:** Only sees backward-looking data.
4. **Time Cost:** Every question requires someone to compile a response.

### Success Statement

> "I have a one-page summary that answers every question the board will ask about AP operations."

### Key Features for This Persona

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Executive summary report | One page, all answers |
| QBR report | Quarterly deep-dive |
| Trend charts | Direction at a glance |
| ROI waterfall | Where savings came from |
| Scheduled email digest | Arrives without asking |
| Actions & results section | Accountability visible |
